Canopy interpretation
=====================

Unzip your assignment into a private folder. Open viewer.html in Chrome with
JavaScript enabled. Use a mouse or trackpad to trace. Enter your assigned code
and use the Case menu to move through the assignment. Work independently and use only the imagery
in the packet. Keep the project repository and other readers’ work closed.
NAIP imagery provided by USDA Farm Service Agency.

The columns show the earlier and later acquisition dates. The upper row is RGB.
The lower row is the same near-infrared/red/green composite at both dates.
Both dates use a nominal 0.6 m grid. Registration notes describe displacement
measured on features in the parent window, where available. The 2025 imagery
is averaged to that grid. No extra shift is applied. Tile dates have limited
flight-strip precision. Small position, color and shadow differences can be
image effects.

The dashed square is the 120 m scoring area. Use the full supplied context to
trace across it when a crown or loss component continues past the dashed line.
Follow the visible trace as far as the crop permits and flag continuation past
the crop. The square lacks the 120 m of surrounding context proposed for later
evaluation. Toggle the scoring boundary and traces for a clean image view.

1. On each date, trace overhead tree crowns visible within the scoring square
   and the visible continuation of anything that crosses it. Trace unknown
   areas on the corresponding date if shadows, leaf state, seams, missing
   data or other obscuration prevent a reliable interpretation of that date.
   Treat other readable areas as noncrown. Use multiple polygons if necessary.
   For an interior hole, trace pieces that leave the hole open.
2. If displacement or another date-to-date difference makes the transition
   unclear even when the individual dates are readable, use Unknown change on
   the early-date footprint. Trace the part of each early crown that is clearly
   absent later on the Visible loss layer. Keep separate loss fragments at any
   size you can interpret. The two dates show visible cover. Leave the cause
   and exact removal date undetermined.
3. Select a trace layer, click vertices on its date's RGB or NIR view, and
   choose Finish polygon. The last vertex is automatically joined to the first.
   Use Undo vertex during drawing. To correct a saved trace, remove it and
   redraw. Inspect the traces and the unmarked views before finishing the case.
4. Mark every case Read complete, Unreadable or Skipped. Use Read complete
   for a finished read even if the loss mask is empty. For Unreadable or Skipped,
   clear all geometry, confidence and the crop flag. Enter active minutes
   for readings and unreadable cases, plus any time spent on skipped cases.
   Enter confidence for completed reads. In Reasons, explain unreadability
   or locate unknown areas or crop truncation by date and approximate position.
5. Keep Chrome downloads in a private folder. Download return when finished
   and send the JSON only to the coordinator through your agreed one-to-one
   route. For a break, download a draft and use Resume from file on the same
   assignment. Keep your final copy until the coordinator verifies receipt.

Adjudicator only
----------------

The adjudication assignment contains cases flagged by the comparison. Use
Original traces to inspect one reader at a time. Use the Trace menu to switch
which original layer is shown. Original overlays are dashed cyan and read-only.
Hide them as needed and trace your own decision.
Record the rationale, disposition and active adjudication minutes. Resolved
requires Read complete. Choose Unresolved when the imagery cannot settle the
disagreement. For Unreadable or Skipped, clear geometry, confidence and the
crop flag.
Return your separate JSON privately to the coordinator.
