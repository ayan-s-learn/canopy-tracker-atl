# Astra project reviews

Astra leads recurring reviews at agreed **progress milestones**, using the evidence so far to decide what to detail next. This is the cumulative review record; [checklist.md](../checklist.md) holds live task state and the next review. Reviews are agent work and do not require a human meeting unless a specific owner decision is needed. See the [glossary](glossary.md) for project terminology.

## When and how to review

1. **Trigger:** hold AR01 after T01–T04 finish their acceptance checks. Each review names the next review ID and an observable trigger, such as a defined set of inquiries completing or a development comparison becoming ready. Bring a review forward when a blocker or new evidence materially changes the next decisions. Record the reason; unfinished tasks retain their real status. No calendar schedule or automatic wakeup is implied.
2. **Prepare:** the integration owner gathers a compact packet of linked task results and revisions, passed/failed checks, coverage and uncertainty, blockers/human-action IDs, resource and recovery limits, and proposed next questions. Use existing records; do not duplicate logs or rerun completed inquiries for the meeting.
3. **Review with Astra:** inspect the evidence, challenge assumptions, decide what is supported, and choose the next bounded round of useful work. Record alternatives, consequential tradeoffs and unresolved objections. Name the actual model and review date; never label a Sol-only review as an Astra review. If Astra is unavailable, save the packet and pending status while authorized work continues; its availability is not an approval gate.
4. **Integrate:** append the outcome below and update the checklist and affected briefs. Detail only work ready to launch: objective, inputs/dependencies, outputs, acceptance checks, ownership, bounds and evidence location under [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). Identify parallel-ready work, follow-ups to unresolved findings, and the next review trigger. Keep later work as sketches. Integrate through the normal branch/review process.

Reviews guide priorities and develop the next briefs; they add no approval gate. Workstream owners may start useful in-scope work between reviews, including coordinated work across teams, under the existing interface, evidence and authority rules. When the next trigger is reached, the integrator initiates the review at the next active session rather than waiting for a user reminder. Already authorized work and healthy jobs may continue while the review is prepared. If no active agent or supported wakeup exists, record the exact resume action rather than promising unattended review.

An Astra planning review does not itself pass a research gate. Preserve protected evaluation, independent review/reproduction and owner authority. If Astra helped design an approach, obtain a separate reviewer for the required independent challenge; agreement at a review is not quantitative validation.

## Review record

Use stable IDs (`AR01`, `AR02`, …). For each completed review, retain: date/model and contributors; triggering milestone and evidence revisions; findings and unresolved objections; decisions and reasons; next ready task IDs with linked briefs and owners or `unclaimed`; carried blockers and human actions; next review ID/trigger; integration revision. Add dated amendments rather than silently rewriting previous decisions. Pending entries must not imply a meeting occurred.

### AR01 — Feasibility findings and next work

- **Status:** planned; no review held. Preparation/integration owner: current checklist integration owner. Review lead: Astra.
- **Trigger:** T01–T04 complete; bring forward if a material blocker or finding changes the shared direction. A negative inquiry can be complete under its brief without passing G1; a blocked item remains blocked.
- **Evidence to bring:** imagery suitability/access (T01), boundary/parcel/GT findings (T02), permit coverage/semantics (T03), and environment/storage evidence (T04), with checks and outstanding limitations.
- **Decisions to resolve:** viable study interval/region, remaining access or recovery gaps, which reference/preparation/linkage work can start, and what evidence the next round must produce. No method or feasibility conclusion is predetermined.
- **Expected outcome:** recorded decisions, a small set of detailed parallel-ready briefs with dependencies and acceptance checks, carried blockers, and an explicit AR02 trigger. Future sketches may be split, replaced or deferred with their evidence preserved.
- **Resume action when due:** assemble the packet, invoke Astra for AR01, then integrate its recorded outcome. AR02's trigger is intentionally unset until AR01 has evidence to choose it.
