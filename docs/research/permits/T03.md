## Date and linkage rules

- For Office of Buildings cases, start with the original submission date and check transition provisions. Assess Parks records and emergency cases under their applicable provisions. City Planning lists the April 13, 2023–December 31, 2025 version and the version effective January 1, 2026. The City also dates a pre-submission arborist meeting requirement for projects affecting trees to June 25, 2025. Earlier cases and any 2019 fallback need the applicable archived text. [City ordinance versions](https://www.atlantaga.gov/government/departments/city-planning/nature-urban-ecology/tree-protection-ordinance-rewrite) · [City Office of Buildings](https://www.atlantaga.gov/government/departments/city-planning/about-dcp/office-of-buildings)
- Preserve submission, posting, arborist decision, issuance, expiry, amendments, appeal and any actual work dates separately. The October 2023 and October 2025 [sample imagery](../imagery/T01.md) bounds when the loss became visible. Test compatibility against the authorization's effective dates and location. A building status or status-update date alone supplies context. Orange postings mark applications and yellow postings indicate preliminary approval. [City posting guide](https://www.atlantaga.gov/government/departments/city-planning/nature-urban-ecology/arborist/arborist-sign-postings)
- Check emergency documentation as well as permits. Under §158-109(1) in the 2023 ordinance, verbal approval can precede removal and the application. The qualifying after-hours procedure calls for next-working-day contact and documentation within five working days of removal, with a permit exemption. Verify the applicable version and the recorded sequence for each case. [City code supplement, §158-109](https://www.atlantaga.gov/home/showdocument?id=67054&t=638990066293839561)
- Link BB/LD and BA records by the authoritative record relationships and reconcile site/parcel identity and vintage. Resolve overlapping revisions, appeals and parcel matches before assigning a match. Reserve unmatched for a documented search of the relevant covered sources. Keep missing coverage, conflicting versions and unresolved joins ambiguous. Record authorization and actual removal separately.
